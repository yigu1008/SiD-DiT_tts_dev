## 6. Deeper probe from records (23:30; `scripts/ls6_interim_report.py`, full output `results/ls6_interim_report.txt`)
**jevact 2x2 — where the -0.45 comes from.** Paired Jev-random on HPSv3: selected trajectory **-0.451** (3/9), oracle best-of-11
**-0.511** (2/9), pool mean over all 11 trajectories **-0.066** (3/9). Both policies generate pools of the same average quality;
random's pool has a higher *maximum*. Selection (rank3) loses 0.16 (Jev) / 0.22 (random) to the oracle, so the selector is not the
problem. Action usage explains the tail: Jev concentrates on CFG 3.5-5.0 (81% of key-step actions; 7.0 only 2%) and two wordings
(w0 28%, w2 38%; w5-w9 ~0%), changing action 3.6x per trajectory; random spreads uniformly (CFG 7.0 13%, all wordings) with 6.8
switches. The one large loss (q0041: 11.18 vs 14.30) is a high-CFG trajectory random stumbled on. Jev costs +127 s/prompt (72 Jev
+ 11 VLM calls). Selected IR -0.12 (4/9) and PickScore -0.06 (4/9) point the same way.

**Jev decisions (n=648 panels of 3, sheet order permuted).** Display position chosen 40% / 41% / **19%** — the third panel is
picked half as often although content is shuffled (original candidate 38/32/30%): a position effect in Jev-Omni's 3-panel
judgement. Confidence is modest: max prob mean 0.568, median 0.500 (uniform = 0.333), flat across key steps (0.53-0.61).

**ec_v2w — best-of-k is the realistic use, and it changes the picture only for the worst noise.**
| noise | mode | best-of-6 vs anchor | vs fresh median noise | beats fresh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| best | early / full / late | -0.44 / -0.42 / -0.79 | +1.47 / +1.49 / +1.12 | 100 / 100 / 89% |
| worst | early / full / late | **+3.03 / +2.90 / +1.88** | **-0.11 / -0.24 / -1.26** | 56 / 56 / 11% |
On a good noise even the best of 6 action variants is below the anchor (actions only subtract). On a bad noise the best of 6 variants
(336 NFE) recovers +3.0 but still only ties ONE fresh median draw (56 NFE, 56% win). By CFG: 7.0 is -0.5 in early/full, 4.0-4.5 are
the only consistently positive bands (+0.3..+0.7); late control is flat in CFG.
